03 — Solar

Solar Water Heaters

Pressurized solar water heating systems from Ultrasun, Chromagen, Kwikot & Solahart that eliminate or drastically reduce the cost of heating water for your home, rental, hotel or institution.

A solar water heater typically pays for itself within 2–3 years through KPLC savings. After that it is essentially free hot water for 15–25 years.

With a 300L system, most families cut their monthly KPLC bill by up to 60%. Rental landlords often see complete ROI within 18 months.
