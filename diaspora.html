The Reality

What Every Diaspora Landlord Knows

😤

The job "done" that wasn't

Your caretaker says it's fixed. Your tenant says it's still leaking. You're 5,000 miles away with no way to know who's right.

💸

Quoted one price, charged another

Contractors know you're abroad. Quotes inflate. Invoices grow. And you can't walk around with them to check the work.

📵

Silence when you need answers

You send a WhatsApp. You wait two days. Your tenant is on the phone daily. You still don't know what's happening.

📋

No records, no history

What was fixed three years ago? What's the boiler age? Which contractor did the last roof? Nobody knows. Property history disappears.

Our Process

How We Work for Diaspora Landlords

We've structured everything around the reality that you're not in the country.

1

WhatsApp First

Message us your situation. We respond within 4 hours during business hours (EAT). We'll ask the right questions and tell you exactly what we think is needed — honestly.

2

We Assess, You Approve

We visit your property, assess the issue, and send you a written quote with photos before we touch anything. No work starts without your explicit approval.

5

Quarterly Video Inspection

On our monthly retainer, we conduct a full video walkthrough of your property each quarter. Sent directly to you. You'll know your property better than your caretaker does.

Questions & Answers

Questions Diaspora Landlords Ask

These come up every time. We'd rather answer them upfront than have you wonder.

How do I know the work was actually done?

You'll receive photos, video walkthroughs, and a written report within 24 hours. For retainer clients, we conduct formal quarterly video inspections. We also welcome tenant confirmation — just give us their number.

What if my caretaker or tenant is managing things and you disagree?

We work directly with you. If there's a conflicting instruction between you and a local manager, we follow your direction and document the disagreement. We don't take sides — we report to you.

What if something goes wrong with the repair?

All workmanship carries a 30-day guarantee. If the same issue recurs within 30 days, we return and fix it at zero cost — and send you an updated report.

Can you help me find tenants or manage the whole property?

We handle maintenance and inspections. For tenant-finding and rent collection, we can refer you to trusted agents we've worked with. We'll be honest about where our expertise ends.
