FAQ

Pricing Questions

Still unsure? WhatsApp us and we'll give you a straight answer — no sales pressure.

Ask on WhatsApp
Are these prices fixed?

Labour ranges vary by scope and access. The call-out fee (KES 1,500) is always fixed. We quote before we start — no surprise invoices. If we quote a fixed price, that price stands regardless of how long it takes us.

What's included in the +20% parts margin?

It covers our sourcing time, warranty handling, and delivery. You see our supplier receipt. If you can source parts cheaper, tell us — we'll use yours. We'd rather lose a small margin than have you feel taken advantage of.

Do you work for free if the job takes longer than quoted?

If we quote a fixed price, that price stands regardless of how long it takes us. We absorb overruns, not you. The only exception is if the scope of work changes materially — in which case we tell you before proceeding, not after.
